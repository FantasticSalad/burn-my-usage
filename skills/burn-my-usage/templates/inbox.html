How this works. Your answers save the moment you tap. The next run reads them before it does anything else, acts on them in your repos and refills this inbox.

Approving a branch approves the exact commit shown on its card. The next run merges it into your local default branch after the tests pass. Nothing is pushed to a default branch, deployed or published from here or by a run.

Deferred items come back on the next run. Ideas wait for as long as you like: Yes queues one for the next run, Later keeps it at the bottom of the Ideas tab, and No closes it. Only you can open or answer this inbox.

Things you can type in Claude Code

  • /burn-my-usageA full run: acts on your answers, merges what you approved, works the queue and refills this inbox. Never deploys or pushes a default branch.
  • /burn-my-usage inboxRecord your answers now. No building and no merges; those wait for the next full run.
  • /burn-my-usage planA dry run: shows what the next run would work on and changes nothing.
  • /burn-my-usage statusUsage so far this week, open branches and what's waiting here.
  • /burn-my-usage stopEnd the run in that session and report what was left mid-flight.
  • BURN IT ALLTyped exactly like that, in the run's session: that run, or the next one started there within the hour, goes to 100% of your weekly limit instead of your stop line. Turn paid extra usage off first. Only you can arm it.
  • /burn-my-usage setupFirst-time setup, or fill in what's missing: repos, stop line, push policy, this page. It asks before changing anything.
